What’s included in our commercial cleaning services

Our commercial cleaning programs are tailored to each facility and can include, but are not limited to:

  • Trash removal, liner replacement, and waste consolidation
  • Dusting of desks, surfaces, fixtures, and accessible ledges
  • High-touch point cleaning and disinfecting (doors, handles, switches, railings)
  • Restroom cleaning, sanitizing, and replenishment of consumables as requested
  • Floor care including vacuuming, sweeping, and damp mopping
  • Breakroom and kitchen surface cleaning including counters, sinks, and tables
  • Spot cleaning of interior glass, entry doors, and partitions
  • Light organization and straightening of common areas
  • After-hours or overnight cleaning to minimize disruption

Additional services can be added based on your needs, including periodic deep cleaning, detail work, or special requests.

We work with a wide range of commercial clients, including:

How pricing works

Commercial cleaning pricing is customized and based on several factors, including square footage, frequency of service, scope of work, facility layout, number of restrooms and common areas, and access considerations.
